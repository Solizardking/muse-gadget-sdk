CHOOSE YOUR COMPANION

A few steps to make it yours.

  1. 01 / YOUR WALLET

    Connect and sign in on your phone

    Open the wallet dashboard and choose your installed wallet. Then press Sign in to Pocket Wallet and approve the displayed message.

    On Android Chrome, choose Use Installed Wallet. On iOS, open the dashboard in a compatible wallet browser. Desktop works with a Wallet Standard extension.

    Open wallet dashboard ↗

    Sign-in allows balance reads and transaction preparation. Every trade still needs your approval in your wallet.

  2. 02 / YOUR COMPANION SESSION

    Give your companion a wallet connection

    After signing in, choose Download companion config in the dashboard. Move the downloaded config.toml to ~/.config/pocket-wallet/config.toml on the Linux account that will run your companion.

    chmod 600 ~/.config/pocket-wallet/config.toml

    The export uses this dashboard’s HTTPS API origin. Keep the config private: its scoped session lasts one hour and cannot submit transactions. Sign in again and export a fresh config when it expires.

  3. 03 / THE MUSE SDK

    Prepare and install on Linux

    Run these commands from your Pocket Wallet checkout, with the Muse SDK checkout beside it at ../muse-gadget-sdk. Adjust --source if your SDK is elsewhere.

    Your Linux Pi or computer needs BlueZ, the distro’s python3-dbus and python3-gi, and an account with access to system D-Bus and /var/lib/musegadget.

    python3 scripts/prepare-linux-sdk.py --source ../muse-gadget-sdk/linux --output build/muse-linux-token
    python3 -m venv --system-site-packages build/muse-linux-token/.venv
build/muse-linux-token/.venv/bin/pip install ./build/muse-linux-token ./linux

    Configure SDK_TOKEN in a private .env.local on that device. This SDK_TOKEN credential is separate from your wallet session and the credentials created by Muse pairing.

    Use a private env file containing only the SDK credential on your Pi. Provider secrets stay on the backend. Follow the SDK’s installer instructions for device-state and D-Bus permissions.

    Read the Muse SDK setup guide ↗
  4. 04 / PAIR WITH MUSE

    Confirm your companion in the app

    node --env-file=.env.local scripts/muse-sdk.mjs pair \
  --sdk build/muse-linux-token --python build/muse-linux-token/.venv/bin/python

    In Muse on your phone, open Settings → Devices → Developer mode. Find MuseGadgetXXXXXX and confirm pairing in the app.

    Run pairing on the Linux device with BLE access. The wallet dashboard’s config export is a separate step and does not pair the device with Muse.

  5. 05 / STAY CONNECTED

    Start the connection loop

    node --env-file=.env.local scripts/muse-sdk.mjs run \
  --sdk build/muse-linux-token --python build/muse-linux-token/.venv/bin/python \
  --run-as your-linux-account

    Replace your-linux-account with the account holding your companion config. A successful connection reports Noise session established and registered with the Muse in the SDK logs.

    A real Pi-to-phone BLE connection has not yet been verified in this project. This page reports backend configuration; it cannot confirm a physical device connection.

BOARD PORT REQUIRED

Handheld pairing is still in development

The Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-1.28 needs GC9A01A display and CST816S touch drivers, network integration, an ESP-IDF v6.0.1 build, flashing, and verified boot and touch behavior.

There is no working handheld pairing build to download yet. The 1.75-inch AMOLED profile targets a different board.

Before hardware pairing

Keep SDK_TOKEN in an ignored private build config. It is compiled into firmware, so firmware containing it must stay private.

A physical press opens phone review within ten seconds. Voice and remote commands cannot act as a physical press or approve spending.

WHEN YOU COME BACK

Reconnect safely

Session expired? Sign in and export a fresh config. Wallet account changed? Reconnect. Quote expired? Request a fresh quote. If a submission is uncertain, check your wallet history before starting another trade.

Keep your keys with you

Private keys stay in your installed wallet. Provider keys stay on the backend. Your companion session belongs in its private config.
